Joez Better Trademark Styling for the Registered and Trademark Symbols

Behavior:

Standalone ®, ®, ™, and ™ are styled to appear superscripted.

Example:

  • This is a test of symbols: ®, ®, ™, and ™ in text
  • BrandName® and BrandName® should appear superscripted
  • ProductName™ and ProductName™ should also appear superscripted
